The Sickle: 13 &M3, 120'
Locality Narrow Neck

A rather good wet weather climb start - red wall with yellow roof 30 yds before P. wall.

1. 70' (crux). 15' over SQ. block to ledge, jam runner. Continue up corner to another ledge - peg runner (useless bolt - should be chopped). 10' to scrubby ledge - peg runner. Airy traverse 20' to right. 10' up small gully to below roof - peg anchor.

2. 50'. 2 threads, 1 jam, 5 pegs, across corner & over roof, tricky mantle-shelf onto ledge. Peg runner, over bulge & up to tree anchor as for 2nd anchor Pawn Wall.

Kieth Bell
Howard Bevan

Pandora: 11, 160'
Locality Pulpit Hill

Good climb. Up wall & arete - hard to start. 
Start small corner capped by fern at 12'.

1. 50'. Up 7' below fern in crack. Swing out right, around nose, up it, then back into crack. Peg runner. Straight up crack to bolt on ledge.

2. 70' (crux). Right 5' to arete. Up arete to loose blocks on ledge. Up slight overhang then up wall above tending right to tree anchor.

Howard Bevan

Aries: 16, 170'
Locality Pulpit Hill

Must surely be one of the classics of the cliff. As the name implies an airy traverse across a bloody magnificent wall. A confident leader & second are required.
Start 50' left of Sagittarius. Flared crack full of choss.

1. 70'. 35' up crack to ledge. Bridge up gully behind, swing onto right wall. Up it to fern covered ledge (small) - jam or bollard anchor (poor).

2. 100' (crux). 6' to ledge, either: 
	a) climb onto ledge & edge right along it to ramp, feeling the cliff pushing & gravity attracting all the way (You have to be at least 6'2" tall).
	b) Hand traverse along ledge to ramp, bloody delicate mantleshelf onto it.
	
	Desperate either way. Up 10' above ramp bollard runner - reverse down to ledge & drift across to nose peg runner. Up nose under roof, swing out onto left wall. Up this to tree anchor as for Sagittarius 2.
	
Kieth Bell
Howard Bevan
20-9-69

Gates of Janus: 17, 185'
Locality Mt Boyce

A direct climb - strenuous layback.
Start 30' to right of Gently Mine, on nose.

1. 105' (crux). 35' up arete to small ledge at base of impressive layback crack. Then follows a strenuous 40' of laybacking to a small roof, with a peg runner at 14' & a small resting place at 25'. A 2" bong placed in roof enables one to rest before swinging right, out & around roof, then up a steep wall to a bollard runner. Traverse left 10' to anchor 1 of Gently Mine.

2. 80'. As for Gently Mine.

Kieth Bell
Howard Bevan

Copernicus: 17, 155'
Locality Medlow Bath

Far harder than appears from below.
Start at corner below big rock.

1. 75' (crux). Up crack to ledge at 20'. Up, swing left around small roof and up to another ledge 35'. Up 10' to smooth buldge with ramp on left hand side. Step across corner onto ramp. Place peg (hard) & use for handhold. Climb up to roof - peg runner. Hand traverse gymnastically leftwards to arete - good stance. Peg anchor.

2. 80'. Diagonally right up juggy wall towards gap in smooth buldge. Over buldge, slightly right & straight up wall to top. Bush anchor.

Serenety: 18&M5, 470'
Locality: SE - Opposite Govetts Leap Lookout, Blackheath

A serious undertaking - no means of retreat

1. 35'. Move up 5' from ledge to a small traverse line leading right. Kick steps & claw ones way across steep mud to bushes. Swing up through bushes to rock. 15' up rock to top of pedestal. Peg anchor.

2. 55'. Move up 10' free, then up crack 30' with jams for aid. A few free moves, then on bongs, pegs, jams & bollard to roof & recess. Belay in slings - peg anchor.

3. 50'. Up right hand side of flake on 2 jams & a peg. Free moves to ledge, jam up narrow chimney for a few feet, then 2 pegs, 2 bongs jams & pegs to in-cut ledge below overhang. Belay in slings. Jam anchor. (See note).

4. 80'. Up for 40' on pegs & jams to small ledge. 20' free up sloping corner to roof - bong runner. 3 jams for aid around loose block in roof - mantle shelf onto blocks. 20' up corner to foothold stance below scrubby ledge. Peg anchor.

5. 125' (crux?). Free from belay onto scrubby ledge. Up crack above on jams till crack widens. Free to small roof choked with loose boulders, small cracker & peg for aid (desperate - aid v. hard to place). Free moves to small stance. Peg runner then free over a series of small bulges to good ledge - jam anchor.

6. 100'. Up sloping corner through small break in overhangs. Two pegs for aid. Traverse rotten groove to ledge & small tree runner. Diagonally left for a few moves then up and back right to chossy groove. Peg for aid. Up groove for 25' to big broken ledge. Tree anchor.

7. 25'. Broken scrambling to top.

Kieth Bell
Howard Bevan

Crucifixion: 18, 800'
Locality Bungles

Start left hand side of scrubby ledge 150' up - easy scramble via gully on left.

1. 50'. Move up, left along ramp type weakness. Bolt anchor on foothold stance.

2. 70'. Up above for 20', bolt runner. Diagonally right for 25' to bolt runner. Continue along same line to bolt anchor foothold stance.

3. 80'. Diagonally right 30'. Up 10' to bolt runner. Up then left to little ledge & bolt anchor.

4. 80'. Diagonally right for 35', along ramp to bolt runner. Around a small nose. Continue along same line to bolt anchor - foothold stance.

5. 90'. Upwards & leftward towards a scoop, then easier up to a good ledge 5' long & 1' wide. Poor peg anchor.

6. 120' (crux). You are now on the right of a shallow gully 40' high. Climb up 30' to near its top. The next 100' of wall is near vertical &/or overhanging. Step across gully & you will see weakness in wall above. Go up for 80' to bolt anchor & foothold stance.

7. Continue up to ledge below the chimney - poor knob anchor. 

8,9,10 300' easy climbing up the chimney, so to slabs & finally on slabby buttress followed to top.

John Ewbank
John Worral
8-9-66
